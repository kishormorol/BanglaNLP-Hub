# BanglaNLP Hub

A community-maintained catalog of Bangla (Bengali) NLP resources: **{{papers}} papers, {{datasets}} datasets, {{models}} models, and {{tools}} tools** across {{tasks}} tasks, browsable by task, venue, year and license.

Bangla is spoken by roughly 240 million people and is still treated as low-resource in practice, largely because the work that exists is scattered across venues, personal pages and dead links. This index makes it countable and searchable, so you can see what exists before rebuilding it.

Every entry is link-verified in CI and maintained through pull requests.

- Catalog as a dataset: [kishormorol/bangla-nlp-catalog](https://huggingface.co/datasets/kishormorol/bangla-nlp-catalog)
- Source and contributions: [github.com/kishormorol/BanglaNLP-Hub](https://github.com/kishormorol/BanglaNLP-Hub)
